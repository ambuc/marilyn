The Art Students League of New York Gala Award Sculpture

Ms. Friedman was commissioned to cast a bronze edition
of one of her sculptures as the award presented to
Honorees at the League’s Annual Gala.

2022 - 2025 Honorees include Faith Ringgold, Larry Poons,
Beth Rubin De Woody, Louise Nevelson (In Memorium), James Little;
150th Anniversary Honoree: The Robert Rauschenberg Foundation
